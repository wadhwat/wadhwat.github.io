## Project brief

MillburnX was my introduction to engineering as a team sport. Across two FTC seasons, I
worked on the robot as a builder and coder while also leading sponsorship and outreach.
The team reached the New Jersey State Championship finals twice and earned the first-place
Inspire Award at its league championship.

## What it adds to this portfolio

The college projects on this site show deeper technical ownership. Robotics shows where
the habits began: building under competition deadlines, integrating work across
subsystems, and explaining the engineering well enough to earn support outside the team.
